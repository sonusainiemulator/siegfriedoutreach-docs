## 🎥 AI Avatar & Talking Video Studio (`/ai-avatar`)

The **Siegfried Outreach AI Avatar & Video Studio** empowers businesses, founders, and healthcare providers to produce high-definition, photorealistic talking presenter videos with lip-synchronized speech in minutes—without cameras, microphones, studio lighting, or complex editing software.

Integrated directly with **Hermes Domain Skills**, the video studio allows you to transform specialized knowledge (such as mental health guidance, STEM burnout coaching, and cognitive reframing) into viral vertical videos formatted specifically for **Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest Video Pins**.

    ### 📍 Step 2: Render in AI Talking Video Studio (`/ai-avatar`)
    1. **Mode**: Choose `TEXT TO VIDEO` or `IMAGE TO VIDEO` (upload your custom portrait or digital twin).
    2. **Choose Character Face**: Select an empathetic presenter preset or your own generated model from the Asset Gallery.
    3. **Select Aspect Ratio**:
       - **9:16 Vertical** *(Recommended)*: For Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
       - **16:9 Landscape**: For YouTube long-form videos and webinar training.
       - **1:1 Square**: For LinkedIn and Facebook feeds.
    4. **Select Voice & Engine**:
       - **Engine**: Choose **HeyGen 2.0 Digital Twin** (high-fidelity lip-sync) or **Hedra Character-2** (expressive facial animation).
       - **Voice**: Choose a calm, empathetic voice tone (e.g., ElevenLabs Rachel or Jenny Neural).
    5. **Test & Generate**: Click **Preview Speech** to verify pacing, then click **Generate Video**.

    ### 📍 Step 3: Multi-Channel Dispatch & Scheduling (`/social-media/create-post`)
    1. Go to **Social Media ➔ Create Post** (`/social-media/create-post`).
    2. Select your target video channels: **Instagram (Reels), TikTok, YouTube (Shorts), Facebook, and Pinterest (Video Pins)**.
    3. Attach your generated MP4 video directly from your library.
    4. Click **AI Magic Assistant** to auto-generate engaging captions and relevant hashtags (e.g. `#MentalHealth #StudentBurnout #Resilience #Psychology`).
    5. Schedule for your audience's peak engagement hours or publish immediately!
