# 📊 AI Slide Maker (`/ai-slide-maker`)

Create presentation pitch decks and social carousel slides in seconds.

<Tabs items={['English Guide', 'हिंदी गाइड']}>
  <Tab value="English Guide">
    ### 🌟 Features:
    - **Topic-to-Presentation**: Type a prompt like *"10 Growth Hacks for SaaS Startups"* and receive 8-10 professionally designed slides.
    - **LinkedIn Carousel Export**: Export slides directly as multi-page PDF formatted for 100% engagement on LinkedIn and Instagram.
    - **Visual Themes**: Modern dark gradients, clean minimal light, corporate blue, and vibrant startup palettes.
    - **Slide Customization**: Edit slide titles, bullet points, icons, and layout in the visual editor.
  </Tab>

    - लिंक्डइन और इंस्टाग्राम कैरोसेल के लिए डायरेक्ट PDF डाउनलोड करें।
