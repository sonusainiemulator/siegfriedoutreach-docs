# ✨ AI Writing Assistant (`/ai-writing-assistant`)

A specialized copy generation suite featuring 50+ pre-built marketing templates for social media, ad copy, email marketing, and e-commerce.

<Tabs items={['English Guide', 'हिंदी गाइड']}>
  <Tab value="English Guide">
    ### 📝 Available Template Categories:
    - **Social Media**: Viral Instagram captions, LinkedIn thought leadership posts, Twitter/X threads, TikTok video hooks.
    - **Marketing Ads**: Facebook primary text & headlines, Google Responsive Search ad copy, TikTok scripts.
    - **Email Marketing**: High open-rate subject lines, cold outreach emails, promotional newsletters.
    - **E-Commerce**: Compelling Amazon/Shopify product descriptions and benefit bullet points.

    ### 🚀 How to Generate Copy:
    1. Go to **AI Tools ➔ AI Writing Assistant** (`/ai-writing-assistant`).
    2. Choose your template (e.g. *LinkedIn Post* or *Facebook Ad Copy*).
    3. Enter product name, key benefits, and target audience.
    4. Click **Generate Copy** to receive 3-5 distinct creative variations.
  </Tab>
