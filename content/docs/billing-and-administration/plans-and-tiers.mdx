## 📦 Plans & Quota Management

Administer workspace tiers, credit allowances, and feature access permissions under **Plans** (`/plans`).

<Tabs items={['English Guide', 'हिंदी गाइड']}>
  <Tab value="English Guide">
    ### 📊 Quota Controls
    - **Post Scheduling Limits**: Set monthly publishing quotas per connected account.
    - **AI Generation Credits**: Allocate monthly allowances for images, reels, and captions.
    - **Team Seat Management**: Control maximum invited team members and role restrictions.
  </Tab>
