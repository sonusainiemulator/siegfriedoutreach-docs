## Mail Setup & Bot Preferences

Configure your sending domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC records) and connect SMTP gateways (Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, or custom cPanel SMTP) in **Campaign Hub ➔ Settings ➔ Mail Setup** (`/campaign-hub/settings/mail-setup`).
