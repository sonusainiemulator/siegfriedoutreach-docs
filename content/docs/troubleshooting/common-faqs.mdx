# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="What video formats and aspect ratios are supported for Reels & Shorts?">
    Siegfried Outreach supports MP4, MOV, and WebM video files in `9:16` vertical aspect ratio. Maximum length is 60 seconds for YouTube Shorts, 90 seconds for Instagram Reels, and 10 minutes for TikTok.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does saving a draft consume my AI credits?">
    No. Saving, updating, and queuing drafts consumes **0 credits**. Credits are only deducted when using AI generation features or executing live publishing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I schedule the same video to post across Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok simultaneously?">
    Yes. Simply select all three platforms in the Post Composer, upload your 9:16 video, and choose your schedule date and time. Our queue worker handles simultaneous multi-network delivery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get my Siegfried MCP API Key for Cursor and Claude?">
    Navigate to **Settings > API & MCP Keys** in your dashboard, click **Generate New Key**, and copy your `sig_live_...` key.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
